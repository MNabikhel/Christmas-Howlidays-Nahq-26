# Key points from the "2026 Tree Gallery" kickoff deck (Tree Gallery 1.pptx)

- Purpose: trees are shown at the PCL Holiday Party, then donated to the University Hospital Foundation (UHF) and auctioned at its **2027** Festival of Trees gala. Think auction-ready, broad appeal.
- Each team receives: a **7.5 ft pre-lit Wimberley Pine** (lights included), a wood base and zip ties, and a Tree Evaluation Sheet (the form) to record what was bought and its approximate value.
- Budget: **$2,000** for decorations.
- Do: secure **every** ornament with pipe cleaners or **floral wire** (trees get moved several times); decorate the full tree so it looks finished from every angle; keep track of purchases for the evaluation sheet.
- Don't: use a **tree skirt** (organizers cover the bases); use PCL logos/branding; theme with other brands or sports teams (e.g. Oilers).
- Expense description format: **Holiday2026_TreeGallery** (PCL Holiday Party code).
- When done: tell organizers, wrap with provided materials, deliver to NAHQ by **Thursday Dec 3**. Special Event Rentals moves trees to the ECC on **Friday Dec 4**.
